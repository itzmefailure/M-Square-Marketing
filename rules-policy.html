Our Rules & Policies

1. VIP 30-Min Event Reels

  • 30-Minute Delivery Guarantee:

    We guarantee the delivery of the final edited cinematic reel exactly 30 minutes after the shoot concludes.

    Hindi: Hum guarantee dete hain ki shoot khatam hone ke theek 30 minute baad aapko final edited reel mil jayegi.

  • Audio & Revisions:

    As this is an express fast-track service, on-the-spot revisions are not available. Minor changes will be processed the next day from our studio.

    Hindi: Yeh ek fast-track service hai, isliye on-the-spot changes nahi hote. Chhote changes agle din studio se karke diye jayenge.

  • Raw Footage:

    Raw (unedited) video files are exclusively provided in our 'Pro' and 'Advance' packages only.

    Hindi: Bina edit ki hui (raw) videos sirf 'Pro' aur 'Advance' plans mein hi di jayengi. Standard plan mein sirf reel milti hai.

2. Web Development

  • Delivery Timelines:

    Pro Level websites will be delivered within 14 Days, and Advance Level websites will be delivered within 30 Days.

    Hindi: Pro level ki website 14 din ke andar aur Advance level ki website 30 din ke andar deliver ki jayegi.

  • Domain & Hosting:

    The first year of Domain and Server Hosting is included in our packages. Standard renewal charges apply from the second year.

    Hindi: Pehle saal ka Domain aur Hosting package mein shamil hai. Agle saal se iska standard renewal charge dena hoga.

  • Revisions & Maintenance:

    Minor updates are free for the first 3 days after the website goes live. After that, any updates or placing new offers will cost ₹199.

    Hindi: Website live hone ke 3 din tak maintenance free rahegi. Uske baad kuch bhi update karwane ya offer lagwane ke liye ₹199 pay karna hoga.

3. Social Media & Ads

  • Ad Budget vs. Management Fee:

    Our packages cover management and design fees only. The actual budget spent on running ads is billed directly to the client.

    Hindi: Package mein sirf hamari management fee hoti hai. Ads (Facebook/Google) chalane ka paisa client ko alag se lagana hota hai.

  • Content Delivery:

    Clients are required to provide raw photos/videos of their business, or a separate photography shoot can be booked with us.

    Hindi: Graphics hum banayenge, par business ki real photos client ko WhatsApp karni hongi ya hamara shoot book karna hoga.

4. Booking & Payments

  • Advance Payment Mandatory:

    A mandatory 50% advance payment is required to start any project or confirm a booking slot. The remaining 50% is due on delivery.

    Hindi: Koi bhi kaam shuru karne ke liye 50% Advance dena compulsory hai. Baaki ka 50% delivery wale din dena hoga.

  • Travel Charges:

    Travel within Banda city limits is free of charge. Events outside city limits will include standard travel charges.

    Hindi: Banda city ke andar shoot pe aana free hai. City ke bahar ke events ke liye travel charge lagega.

  • Refund Policy:

    Once a project has started or digital assets (domains/hosting) have been purchased, the advance payment is strictly non-refundable.

    Hindi: Kaam shuru hone ya domain kharidne ke baad advance payment wapas (refund) nahi hogi.
